Policy and limits

The automated player completed the tutorial through the UI, bought affordable upgrades with an estimated payback under 300 seconds, met expansion investment requirements, claimed earned goals, assigned the starter and trained once, and welcomed eligible managers. New specialists were assigned to empty roles; an existing trained manager was not replaced by a weaker recruit. The run used no research or offline reward and stayed mainly in the newest restaurant.
