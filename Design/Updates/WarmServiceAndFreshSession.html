Idle Restaurant 0.5.1 — warm service and fresh-save pacing

Visual fixes

All eight original Street Kitchen environment prefabs were rebaked: each shade shares its counter centre, with the same geometry in the runtime fallback. All three counters now have three small pendants, including the left Orders counter. Four standing corner lamps remain. Every table has an ivory candle in a green holder with a brass saucer, wick and warm animated flame. Candles are hidden with tables during the four-seat tutorial; flames and their local illumination appear only at night. Reduced motion keeps flames steady.

There are 13 small shadowless point lights at night: four corners, three counter pools and six local candle pools. They are disabled by day. This needs Android device profiling before release; Editor performance does not establish phone performance.

Pacing changes motivated by the opening run

The opening baseline reached four services in about three minutes with earned tickets waiting unused and Team still locked until restaurant two. The free starter now arrives after four completed services. Assignment and training are available immediately. The second restaurant no longer grants a duplicate starter. Paid-with-earned-ticket invitations still require the stated restaurant, service and ticket milestones. Within each rarity, invitations prioritize less-represented roles, so the first three teammates cover Orders, Kitchen and Dining; manager IDs, appearance, stats, ownership and training are preserved.

The play screen now shows the next restaurant cost and service/upgrade progress. Gold readiness markers on Map and Team indicate a ready expansion, useful unassigned teammate or available invitation. Owned managers and the next invite appear at the top of Team; unavailable assignment/training buttons are grey. The progress text received a solid readable background during the live test; this presentation-only adjustment changed no earnings or unlock requirements.

Restaurant prices, service gates, upgrade gates, upgrade effects and ticket prices remain the 0.5 rules: first expansion $3,000; subsequent prices ×1.95; service requirement 8+ceil((index−1)×0.75); improvement requirement 12+min(2×(index−1),60). Invitations now require at least two completed services since the previous welcome, as well as the existing total-service, ticket and restaurant milestones. This was added after the long run exposed two rapid invitations at restaurant three. The final gap is covered by isolated model tests; the 21.5-minute measured run occurred before that final gap correction. The free starter milestone is editable as FamilyBalance.starterServices. These are deliberate restaurant adaptation rules, not verified exact Traffic Flow parity.

How to repeat and edit

The source project stays editable. Shade locations come from RestaurantWorld.CounterPosition; rebake only the eight Street prefabs after changing roof geometry. Candle geometry and night intensity are in RestaurantAtmosphere. RestaurantJourney.nightTransitionSeconds controls the fade, and FamilyBalance controls the earned progression. Tools/Verification/FreshSession.cs is an external Unity script runner harness: run its Main in Play mode, with a 720×1280 Game view and run-in-background enabled. It resets the live model to fresh and restores the prior live model in finally; back up disk saves separately, and restore them after leaving Play mode. Raw captures go under Recordings and are ignored by Git. Stop via StopAllCoroutines to run cleanup. The test is limited to 25 minutes, with an early finish after 20 minutes if three restaurants and three managers have been reached.

Limits and release status

Android APK 0.5.1 (code 8) built successfully with zero errors. ARM64, Android 11 minimum, ZIP integrity and v2 local debug signature verified. No billing or advertising-ID permission. One existing unused-field compiler warning remains. This is a local test APK, not a store-signed release.

The video contains edited, timestamped highlights of actual live Unity play: the final fresh visual recheck for the opening/night view, and earned expansion/starter/Kitchen milestones from the longer session. The rapid fourth invitation is omitted because the final gap rule prevents that burst. It is not an uninterrupted recording of the final balance, with the live game mix. Encoding at 30 fps does not mean the Editor or an Android phone ran at 30 fps. The full long-term balance remains a forecast; no claim is made that it matches Traffic Flow completion time. No physical Android device was tested in this update. Signing/store preparation and real-device graphics, touch, battery and thermal validation remain release work.
