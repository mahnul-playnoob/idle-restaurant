New progressive rules

For the next outlet index n (first expansion n=1), price = rounded(3000 × 1.95^(n−1)); previous outlet must finish 8 + ceil((n−1)×0.75) services and buy 12 + min(2×(n−1),60) total card levels. All requirements are shown on its map card. Cash alone cannot bypass them. Players can return to earlier restaurants while saving.

The second outlet grants a free starter. Subsequent invitations welcome exactly one unowned manager, lowest rarity first in stable catalog order. With m managers owned: cost = 8+2m earned tickets; required total services = 4+4m+floor(0.12m²); open restaurants = min(40,2+floor(m/2)). Tickets alone cannot rush the roster. Training and assignment remain optional. The Dining manager speed bonus now affects the actual eating phase.

Session 1: short first-guest tutorial, two guided improvements, first service reward, explore the map and continue earning toward restaurant 2. Sessions 2–3: welcome and assign the free starter, complete services toward the next individual teammate, improve orders/kitchen/dining. Later sessions work toward district entry and restaurant-specific service and investment milestones. No energy limit or purchases stop continued play. Stars and recurring goals remain available after all outlets open; finite authored content is not infinite unique content.

Presentation changes

  • Square-fit district emblems and eight food silhouettes per district, including separate Sunny Counter burger and Toast and Bean toast/coffee art.
  • Camera yaw zero; roof aligned horizontally; entrance bottom-left and exit bottom-right.
  • Four corner standing lamps and three hanging fixtures per chef counter. Ten fixtures share six shadowless point lights. First completed service fades day to night over four seconds; the following service returns to day. Lights are off in daylight. Saved service parity restores the appropriate time of day.
  • Currency-only action labels. Green affordable upgrade price bars and grey unaffordable cards remain.
  • 71 individually seeded portraits and distinct fictional full names/surnames. Appearance does not affect stats or assign ethnicity to roles.
  • Cooking variations and steam, two-handed plate carrying, pickup reach and eating/bite progress.
  • Persistent Orders/Kitchen/Dining decorations at total stage investment 5,15,30: counter trim/tablet/menu, prep equipment/pans, tablecloths/flowers/placemats. These cosmetics do not alter collision or capacity.
