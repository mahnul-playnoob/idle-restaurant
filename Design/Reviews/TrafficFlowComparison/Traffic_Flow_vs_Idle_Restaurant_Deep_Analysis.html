Traffic Flow
vs Idle Restaurant

Traffic Flow 0.8.1 package, footage, GDD and balance compared with Idle Restaurant 0.3.0’s Android artifact, active Unity game and recorded evidence.

Verdict: Restaurant is a working, original family-game foundation. It shares Traffic Flow’s three-stage idle structure and content counts, but its active progression, economy and repeat loop have been redesigned. It has not reached equivalent presentation polish or verified mobile release readiness.

1. What was actually verified

The APKs were inspected directly for package identity, version, platform requirements, structure and size. The reference DEX was matched to the preserved extraction. GDD paragraphs and workbook cells/formulas were read; no source tuning was changed. Traffic Flow footage and all 450 frames of the current 15-second Restaurant recording were indexed, with meaningful screen states visually inspected. A new Restaurant first-session test ran against the active scene, through real UI raycasts and guest movement.

Testing boundary: no Android phone/emulator was connected. Editor gameplay is not APK gameplay. This review cannot certify phone FPS, touch reliability, thermal behavior, Android suspend/resume or child comprehension. A static package, authored GDD and prior tests are not three independent confirmations of the same behavior. The full original Traffic Flow XAPK archive was not located in Downloads; its preserved base/split APKs and manifest were available.

Base-level error is about 2%. At maximum upgrades it rises to 12–13%. This matters because inactive outlets are paid directly from the forecast while the selected outlet earns through actual guests, and offline rewards use the same forecast. Two independently reproduced rule gaps

  • Dining speed: assigning a level-15 Mythic Dining teammate produces a nominal speed bonus of +240%, but eating stays at 3.8 seconds. Pay increases from 6 to 9 for the dining stage. The team member is useful for pay; its speed component is not applied by Family MealDuration. Affinity also is not consumed by active family equations. UI does not currently expose the full bonus vector, so this is an incomplete mechanic rather than a demonstrated false numeric UI promise.
  • Pending offline cap: at the initial 127.712/min forecast, a 24h absence adds 183,905.39 coins. A second 24h absence without claiming yields 367,810.79 pending. Claiming twice still returns zero on the second claim. The difference is accumulated-cap semantics, not a duplicated claim. Decide whether this generosity is intentional.

5. Progression, difficulty and total playtime

Both games are primarily economic progression games; there is no demonstrated combat, failure score or dexterity challenge. Difficulty comes from choosing investments, recognizing bottlenecks and waiting for affordability/unlocks. Removing ads/IAP is appropriate for the requested edition, but it creates a new balance problem to solve rather than an automatic parity conversion.

Both calculations start with zero cash and use normal steady service sizes, choose the lowest cost per incremental network income, and max all 270 upgrades before opening each next route/outlet. Long-term experience

Restaurant can keep cycling services, repeating goal books and earning non-destructive stars. That supplies practical repeatability, but not unlimited new content. There are still 40 finite authored outlets, the same local level cap, the same common service topology, and a finite research/team roster. After full local maxing, repeated stars mostly increase income and star research rather than reopening a local upgrade journey. Traffic Flow resets local cards and makes rebuild economics steeper, which supplies another investment cycle at the cost of complexity.

For a ten-year-old, retaining earned upgrades is a reasonable choice. Improve lasting variety through visible recipe mastery, earned decorations, new customer patterns and optional challenges; do not equate giant currency values or an unbounded counter with an engaging endless game. These are proposals, not features currently verified.

11. Recommended development sequence

  1. Correct what the player is being told. Fix navigation selection, map wrapping, shortfall labels, teammate effect definitions and income-rate semantics. Keep economy changes separate from visual changes.
  2. Make the opening minute satisfying. Compact the header, enlarge the scene, make food readable, improve guest contacts and add a clear first-service reward moment. Re-run the whole unaided onboarding test after each significant change.
  3. Give each district a reason to exist. Evolve layouts, stations, serving props and signature dish icons. Start with small differences and remeasure the resulting cycle times; cosmetic theme changes should not silently alter income.
  4. Set the intended progression horizon. Define open-all, max-all and repeat-play milestones for the free family edition. Rebuild forecasts using measured production and the same endpoint policy; retain Traffic Flow numbers only as a separately labeled reference.
  5. Validate and prepare distribution. Test named phones and real players, then complete release signing, store assets and diagnostics. No further ad/billing setup is needed for the current direction.
