Editing Idle Restaurant Tycoon

Open the repository in Unity 6000.3.26f1. The production entry point is Assets/Restaurant/Production/RestaurantGame.unity. The earlier concept scene remains available separately.

Balance and content

Select Assets/Restaurant/Production/Data/RestaurantCatalog.asset. Its 40 outlets retain source IDs, capacities, VIP covers, fares, unlock costs, body modifiers, cost scales and stage weights. Current costs are the default. Switch Use New Costs to test the alternate source table. Starter overrides replace route scale and stage weight. The source power table preserves Java double rounding across platforms.

Research nodes, manager definitions, offer contents, offline caps, upgrade costs and service-wave coefficients are in this catalog. Some raw upgrade effects are centralized in Economy.Effect; update its tests if changing those formulas. Prototype values and source-derived values are distinguished in AcceptanceAndParity.json.

Environments

Every outlet has a replaceable environment prefab and signature food slot. Edit Art/Outlet00.prefab through Outlet39.prefab, or assign a custom prefab in the outlet definition. Coordinates use metres: service counters around z=4, dining around z=-2.5 to 1.5, with the camera looking from negative z. Environment geometry never sets logical capacity or prices. Keep paths visually clear and avoid colliders unless an actual interaction needs them.

The bundled scenery batches static meshes by material for runtime rendering. For custom animated scenery, add a RestaurantActorMarker above animated renderers so they are excluded from static batching. Preserve prefab origins and scales when replacing art.

Characters and food

Guest, chef and host prefab slots are on the catalog. Replacement characters need RestaurantActorRig with body, head, left/right arms and legs assigned; tray is optional. The original procedural pose driver animates these parts. Artists may replace this driver with an Animator while preserving the simulation-to-presentation interface. Source character art is original and generated from editable meshes.

SignatureFood0.prefab through SignatureFood39.prefab are separately editable models. Their origin is the plate centre; use roughly 0.6m serving width. They are decorative and never change payouts. Additional menu-side dishes are presentation work, not extra economy items.

Sound and UI

Sound is original synthesized music and event-driven coin/tap/upgrade cues. Replaceable clips and music volume are assigned on RestaurantCatalog. The concept soundtrack is not played during production gameplay. Assign your own imported clips to the catalog audio slots; preserve the existing sound/music settings. UI uses uGUI/TMP. Screens and buttons are built through shared helpers with safe-area anchoring; custom district icons are editable DistrictIcon geometry.

Authoring and verification

  • Restaurant → Production → Content and Economy Workbench: catalog inspector, outlet cost/income preview, acceptance checks and Editor-only unlock/funding tools.
  • Import Reference Catalog reimports source numbers and resolves existing art slots; it replaces numerical outlet/research/manager definitions. Commit artist changes before reimporting.
  • Bake District Prefabs and Bake Characters and Food regenerate supplied art. They are authoring tools, not required for normal play. Commit custom art before regenerating.
  • Run Acceptance Checks checks cost fixtures, wave conservation, research, offline earnings, receipts and save recovery. Test Actual UI Path exercises rendered hit-testing in Play mode.
  • Build Android Development APK creates Builds/IdleRestaurant-Android.apk. Android 11/API30 and ARM64 are configured. No publisher release keystore is bundled.
  • Record Actual Gameplay 15s captures running simulation frames and audio, followed by Tools/EncodeFrames.swift and AddSoundtrack.swift for the MP4.

Save and demo services

Local saves use a checksum envelope, atomic replacement and a backup in Application.persistentDataPath/restaurant-v1.json. Demo offers use demo.* IDs and visibly labelled confirmation; no store is contacted and no money is charged. Demo reward grants are compiled only for Editor/development builds. Production ad, purchase, consent, receipt validation and online analytics remain unconfigured.
