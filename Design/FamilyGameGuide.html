What the player does

Watch a small restaurant serve guests automatically. Spend earned coins on green upgrade cards with separate price bars. Finish services to earn tickets, welcome teammates and open the next restaurant on the map. Explore 40 outlets across Street Kitchens, Comfort Diners, Asian Kitchens, Mediterranean Bistros and Dessert Cafes. Their food, colors, scenery and map emblems follow their district.

A short, progressive welcome

  1. One welcome panel: “Open my restaurant.” No menus to study.
  2. Watch the first guest queue, order, collect a meal, sit and eat. Coins appear through useful service work.
  3. The first completed meal highlights Faster Orders. Other tutorial-locked cards say SOON.
  4. Tap Kitchen, then the highlighted Faster Delivery card. More tables become available.
  5. Complete the first six-guest service; receive coins and a ticket. The map unlocks.
  6. Save for the second restaurant. Team features unlock there. The guide can be skipped; Help stays available.

The live Editor test measured the first paid meal at 24.5 seconds and the first service at 42.9 seconds, including real UI taps and a mid-meal save/reload. These are measured onboarding results, not a promise for every device or strategy.

Logical service and animation

Guests have saved states: arrival → queue → order → pickup → reserved chair → sit → eat → stand → return plate → leave. Cooking has two work positions. Food assembles during preparation and loses portions while eaten. People vary their idle, ordering, cooking, carrying, eating and farewell gestures. Walking animation follows actual movement. Guests never orbit for decoration.

Static chair, table, floor and counter colliders work with guest CharacterControllers. Routes use separate entry, pickup, dining and exit lanes. Chair ownership prevents double booking; sitting and standing use controlled transitions. Saving preserves guest state, chair, meal payment flags and route progress. Reused controllers are disabled during spawn placement before re-enabling collision.

Expected playtime

The documented analytical policy opens restaurant 2 at about 1.3 minutes and all 40 in about 353 minutes. It buys forecast-efficient upgrades and excludes research, teammates, star ranks and offline earnings. This is a conditional model, not six hours of observed gameplay. Different choices and breaks alter pacing. The finite authored content is 40 outlets; continued play reuses those outlets through upgrades, stars, team growth and repeating goals. It does not generate infinite new environments.

Acceptance before public release

  • Install and play on at least two representative Android 11+ midrange phones; verify touch, navigation, safe areas, suspend/resume and a 30-minute service session.
  • Measure actual frame time, memory, battery/thermal behavior and low-memory recovery.
  • Have a ten-year-old try the welcome without verbal help; confirm they can buy upgrades, recognize affordable cards and find the next restaurant.
  • Publisher chooses release signing and completes current store forms and age/content declarations. No billing or ad account is needed for this edition.
