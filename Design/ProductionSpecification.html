Idle Restaurant Tycoon

Production specification — implementation in progress. Android 11+, portrait, 30 FPS target on midrange phones. This document separates confirmed source rules from reconstructed behavior.

Core loop and controls

Guests queue, place orders, receive kitchen fulfilment and complete dining. Cash earned at each stage buys nine upgrades grouped into Orders, Kitchen and Dining. A capacity-limited service wave gives a completion bonus. Open successive outlets, complete district licences, hire managers, research improvements and star maxed outlets to repeat progression. Tap upgrades and navigation; mouse and keyboard are supported for Editor testing.

Environment and progression

40 outlets follow five eight-outlet districts: outdoor Street Kitchens, retro Comfort Diners, lantern-and-bamboo Asian Kitchens, arched Mediterranean Bistros, and pastel Dessert Cafes. Each has original geometry, food and a map emblem. A progressive path communicates prerequisites; all logical capacities remain independent of visible crowd size.

Playtime

The supplied continuous-online reference model estimates Current x1/x2/x6 at 422.76/226.21/97.19 hours; New at 356.38/194.03/88.23 hours. These assume greedy upgrades, all upgrades before the next outlet, and no managers, research or prestige. They are not observed playtimes. Exact customer timing and account effects remain blockers to exact pacing parity.

Monetization proposal and dependencies

Reference-observed permanent x2 / x3 income, improved offline cap/rate, gems and timed cash offers are retained as catalog candidates. Rewarded income boost and gems remain voluntary. No forced interstitial placement was verified. Durations and limits are proposed until verified. Production advertisements, billing, consent, receipt handling, pricing and analytics require publisher service configuration; development simulations must be visibly labelled and unavailable in release builds.
